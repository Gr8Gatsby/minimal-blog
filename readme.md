Minimal Blog
============

Minimal Blog is a calm reading space where new ideas are published in a simple, distraction-free format. The homepage gives a quick introduction, and the Blog page gathers every article in one place so you can browse at your own pace.

How to Use the Site
-------------------
- **Start at Home:** Visit `index.html` for a quick welcome and to see what the site is about.
- **Dive into the Blog:** Head to `blog.html` to browse every post. Each entry includes a short description so you can pick what interests you most.
- **Explore Human Use AI:** The `human-use-ai.html` page collects longer-form thoughts on how people and AI work together.
- **Open Any Post:** Click a title (or image when shown) to read the full article. Posts may include additional recommended reading under "Related" when available.

Tips for the Best Experience
----------------------------
- Add pages to your bookmarks so you can return quickly as new writing appears.
- Most images are optimized for desktop and mobile; if a page seems slow to load, give it a moment—the layout favors visuals in spots.
- Questions or feedback? Reach out through the contact details shared in the posts or via the channel where you discovered the site.
